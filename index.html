CSAT: Time, Speed & Distance

Perfect mathematical solutions to avoid negative marking.

Concept: Relative Speed

1. Objects moving in SAME direction: Relative Speed = (S1 - S2)
2. Objects moving in OPPOSITE direction: Relative Speed = (S1 + S2)

Perfect Solution Method for Train Problems:
Always convert km/hr to m/s by multiplying by (5/18). If a train crosses a platform/bridge, Total Distance = Length of Train + Length of Platform. Formula: Total Distance = Relative Speed × Time.

Polity: Constitutional Bodies

Election Commission of India (ECI) - Article 324

- Multi-member body (Chief Election Commissioner + 2 Election Commissioners).
- Appointed by the President. Removals of CEC are similar to a Supreme Court Judge.
- UPSC Trap: State Election Commissions are separate entities and conduct Panchayat/Municipal elections, NOT state legislative elections.
